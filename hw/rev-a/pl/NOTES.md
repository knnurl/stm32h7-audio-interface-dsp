## The circuit

**Cables.** J_PANEL and J_PJ take their pins from `links.py` (J_PANEL pin 39 open). Everything PR
uses passes straight from J_PANEL to J_PJ: POT_REF, POT1 to POT5, POT_GND, BTN4, BTN5, LED5,
LED6, PANEL_3V3 and GND. The link check (both ends against `links.py`, from KiCad's netlist)
passes for J_PANEL at PL and J_PJ at PL and PR; the full `check_links.py` needs boards D and A.

**Grounds.** GND arrives only through J_PANEL's ground conductors (architecture 7). POT_GND is a
net of its own: pot bottoms and wiper capacitors only, never joined to GND on this board (board D
ties it to the Nucleo's AGND). Nothing on PL touches the front panel electrically: the mounting
holes have no pad, and the pots' and encoders' bracket pads (MP) are left unconnected, so a
bushing touching the panel does not bond panel GND to chassis.

**LEDs (architecture 11.4).** Each LED's anode goes to PANEL_3V3 through its resistor; the
cathode goes to its LEDn line, and the MCU sinks it (active low PWM). The resistor sits on the
anode side so the ribbon conductor carries the LED current back to the MCU pin, not the shared
grounds.

- R = (3.3 V - Vf) / I = (3.3 - 2.0) / 2.5 mA = 520 ohm; E24 **510 ohm** gives 2.55 mA.
- Over an amber Vf spread of 1.9 to 2.2 V: 2.7 to 2.2 mA. The MCU pin's low level at 2.5 mA
  (tens of mV) lowers it slightly [Verify V_OL in DS12919].
- Seven LEDs at 2.55 mA: 18 mA from PANEL_3V3 and into the MCU's pins [Verify the H7's per-pin
  and total sink limits in DS12919, as architecture 11.4 already says].

**Pots (architecture 11.2).** Each pot sits between POT_REF (pin 3) and POT_GND (pin 1), wiper
(pin 2) on POTn with 10 nF to POT_GND right at the pot. Pin 3 is taken as the clockwise end, so
the reading rises clockwise [Verify against the RK09L drawing; swapping 1 and 3 in `design.py`
flips it]. The source impedance seen by the 10 nF is at most 2.5 kohm (wiper at mid travel), a
corner of 1 / (2 pi x 2.5 k x 10 nF) = 6.4 kHz at worst, higher elsewhere: it shunts ribbon pickup
without slowing the knob. D's 1 k and 100 nF do the real filtering.

**Encoders and buttons (architecture 11.3).** Encoder common C and switch S2 to GND; A, B, S1 to
ENCn_A, ENCn_B, ENCn_SW. Buttons BTN1 to BTN3 to GND. Pull-ups, series resistors and filter
capacitors are on board D, so PL carries only switches.

**OLED header (architecture 11.5).** I2C4 (SCL, SDA), PANEL_3V3 and GND and nothing else, with a
ground beside each signal in the cable:

| Pin | Net | | Pin | Net |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PANEL_3V3 | | 2 | GND |
| 3 | I2C4_SCL | | 4 | GND |
| 5 | I2C4_SDA | | 6 | GND |
| 7 | open | | 8 | open |
| 9 | open | | 10 | open |

Decoupling at the header: C8 100 nF 0603 and C9 10 uF 0805 X5R (0805 because a 0603 10 uF loses
much of its capacitance at 3.3 V bias). Pull-ups 4.7 k are at the MCU end. The connector's Value
is `OLED`; it is not a cable in `links.py`, so `check_links.py` ignores it.

**PWR_FLAGs.** GND and PANEL_3V3 arrive by cable and are drawn as power symbols, so each has a
PWR_FLAG on the cables sheet.

## Deviations from the architecture and the brief

1. **OLED header pins 7 to 10 are open**, not more grounds: architecture 11.5 says "nothing
   else". Any of them can become a ground or a reset line later without touching the cable.
2. **Pot and encoder bracket pads (MP) are unconnected** (above). If the owner wants the
   brackets grounded for shielding, they would go to GND only if the bushings are insulated
   from the panel; otherwise leave them.
3. **Encoder footprint is the EC11E** (Alps). EC11 class parts from other makers share the pin
   pattern [Verify the chosen part against it].

## Open owner decisions

1. **LED colours (architecture 11.4).** Default amber for all five (and LED5, LED6 on PR).
   Yellow-green or red work with the same 510 ohm (Vf about 2.0 to 2.2 V; yellow-green at 2.1 V
   gives 2.35 mA). Brightness differs between colours; resistors may need trimming after the
   first board.
   **Blue FX-on LED (D7):** Vf 2.8 to 3.2 V leaves 0.1 to 0.5 V across the resistor from 3.3 V,
   so the current would be ill-defined (about 0.2 to 1 mA with 510 ohm). It needs a 5 V anode
   supply: R = (5.0 - 3.0) / 2.5 mA = 800 ohm, E24 820 ohm, and either a small N-MOSFET or NPN
   on PL driven by LED7 (the MCU's PWM inverted to active high) or the MCU pin in open drain on
   a 5 V tolerant (FT) pin [Verify DS12919 that the pin may sit at 5 V minus the LED's off
   drop]. J_PANEL has no 5 V conductor; pin 39 is the only free one, so the change touches
   `links.py`, board D and PL.
2. **Buttons: B3F-5000 is 12 x 12 mm.** `mechanical_interface.md` section 4 puts FX select, on
   and tap at 9 mm pitch (x = 65, 74, 83) and mute, dim at 10 mm (153, 163). 12 mm bodies do not
   fit. Either the 6 x 6 mm B3F-1000 class (`Button_Switch_THT:SW_TH_Tactile_Omron_B3F-100x`)
   with caps, or about 14 mm spacing. The schematic is the same either way; only the footprint
   in `design.py` changes.
