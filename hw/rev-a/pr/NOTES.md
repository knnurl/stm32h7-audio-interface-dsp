## The circuit

**Cables.** J_PJ and J_HP take their pins from `links.py` (J_HP pins 5 and 6 open, so the cable
adds no second ground path between A and the panel). The link check from KiCad's netlist passes
for J_PJ at PL and PR and J_HP at PR; the full `check_links.py` needs boards D and A.

**Grounds.** GND arrives only through J_PJ, so only through PL and J_PANEL. POT_GND is its own
net and is not joined to GND here. The four headphone nets (HP_L, HP_R, HP_L_RET, HP_R_RET) have
no connection to GND or POT_GND on this board: the returns go to the OPA1622's ground on A only
(architecture 7, board split 3.4). Pot bracket pads (MP) and the mounting holes are unconnected,
so nothing on PR is bonded to the front panel.

**Pots.** Five 10 k linear pots between POT_REF (pin 3, the clockwise end [Verify]) and POT_GND
(pin 1), wiper (pin 2) on POTn with 10 nF to POT_GND at the pot. Worst-case corner with the pot's
2.5 kohm mid-travel source: 6.4 kHz (see PL's notes). RV1 to RV3 are the centre-detent version;
their Value says `10k lin detent`.

**Buttons and LEDs.** BTN4, BTN5 to GND. LED5, LED6: anode to PANEL_3V3 through 360 ohm (plus 150 ohm on board D), cathode
to the LEDn line, which the MCU sinks: (3.3 - 2.0) / (360 + 150) = 2.55 mA (derivation and spread in PL's
notes).

**Phones jack (architecture 12, board split 3.4).**

- Tip T = HP_L, ring R = HP_R, sleeve S = HP_L_RET.
- HP_R_RET joins the sleeve through NT1 (`Device:NetTie_2`), placed at the sleeve pad, so both
  cable nets keep their names in the netlist (checked: HP_L_RET and HP_R_RET are separate nets
  joined only by NT1). Layout: each return runs beside its own signal from J_HP to the jack and
  meets the other only at NT1.
- The switching contacts TN, RN and SN are unconnected: nothing needs a plug-detect.
- The NJ6FD-V body is plastic, so the sleeve does not touch the front panel (the condition for
  the amplifier on board A, architecture 12). It needs a 1.2 to 1.5 mm panel: the spot face
  decided in board split section 2, item 2.

**TVS (architecture 9).** D8 from HP_L and D9 from HP_R to the sleeve (HP_L_RET), right at the
jack: Nexperia PESD12VL1BA, a low-capacitance bidirectional ESD diode in SOD-323, 12 V
stand-off, 15.9 V maximum clamping (distributor listing [Verify the datasheet]); LCSC C38558
[Verify JLC class and stock].

- Stand-off: the OPA1622 runs from +/-12V_ANA and cannot swing past about +/-11 V, under the
  12 V stand-off in both directions, so the diode stays off with any signal. Architecture 9 asks
  for at least 12 V; this meets it with no margin to spare, which is why the swing limit matters
  [Verify the OPA1622's output swing on +/-12 V in SBOS727B].
- Capacitance: the series is sold as low capacitance; the value is not yet read [Verify in the
  datasheet, expected tens of pF]. Driven through A's 1 ohm build-out, tens of pF does nothing in
  the audio band; the OPA1622's capacitive-load stability with the cable plus the TVS is the
  thing to check [Verify SBOS727B].
- Two single diodes rather than a dual in SOT-23 so each sits next to its own pad and the
  return path is the sleeve pad itself.

**PWR_FLAGs.** GND and PANEL_3V3 arrive by cable and are drawn as power symbols, so each has a
PWR_FLAG on the cables sheet. The headphone nets are labels, not power symbols, so ERC asks for
no driver.

## Deviations from the architecture and the brief

1. **Pot bracket pads (MP) are unconnected**, as on PL.
2. **NetTie footprint is the 0.5 mm SMD pad version.** Its copper bridge carries the right
   channel's return current (under about 130 mA peak, the OPA1622's limit); widen the bridge in
   layout if the copper is thinner than about 0.3 mm.
3. **The TVS part is picked here** (architecture 9 named only its class).
