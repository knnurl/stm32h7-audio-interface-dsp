## 1. Power entry (sheet `power_entry`)

Path: J1, switch, F1 (PTC), Q1 (reverse polarity), Q2 (over-voltage cut-off), D3 (TVS), C2 (bulk),
giving 12V_P. Order as arch 2 and 9.

**J1, JST XH 4-way (B4B-XH-A, vertical, 2.5 mm).** Pin 1 the jack's centre (12V_JACK), pin 2 its
sleeve (GND), pins 3 and 4 the power switch; the board joins pin 1 to pin 3, so the switch sits in
series and the harness is two separate 2-wire pigtails, one to the jack and one to the switch.
Why XH: polarised, friction-locking, a stock KiCad footprint, about 3 A per contact against a
0.69 A worst-case input (arch 5.4) [Verify JST's rating], cheap crimp housings. 12V_IN is after
the switch, before protection (the test point arch 14 calls "12V_IN"). The jack's negative is
circuit ground, not chassis (arch 7).

**F1, PTC, 1812, hold 1.1 A, at least 24 to 30 V.** Arch 5.4 asks for hold of at least 1.0 A; the
input is 0.69 A worst, so 1.1 A leaves room for the hold-current derating at about 50 C inside
the case (typically about 80 % of the 25 C figure [Verify the chosen part's curve]). Voltage
rating: the PTC can only trip with an input below the cut-off (above it Q2 carries no current;
reversed, Q1 carries none), so 16 V would do; 24 V or more is asked for margin. **Part not chosen**
(candidates in the Bourns MF-MSMF or Littelfuse 1812L families) [Verify part, rating, stock].

**Q1, reverse polarity: DMP3099L-7** (P-FET, SOT-23, -30 V, Vgs +/-20 V, about 65 mohm at
Vgs = -10 V [Verify DMP3099L datasheet]). Drain to the input, source to 12V_RP, so its body
diode conducts only with the right polarity and the FET then enhances. R1 100 k gate to GND;
D1 BZT52C10 from source (cathode) to gate holds |Vgs| at or below 10 V; at 30 V in, D1 carries
(30 - 10) / 100 k = 0.2 mA. Reversed, the source stays near 0 V, Vgs is about 0, Q1 is off.
Dissipation 0.69^2 x 0.065 = 31 mW.

**Q2, over-voltage cut-off: DMP3099L-7**, source to source with Q1 (12V_RP), drain to 12V_P, so
its body diode blocks forward current when it is off. On by default (R2 100 k gate to GND, D2
clamps Vgs). U1 TL431BIDBZR (0.5 %, pin-out from TI SLVS543S Table 5-1: DBZ 1 cathode, 2 REF,
3 anode, matching the KiCad symbol) compares 12V_RP / (R3 + R4) with 2.495 V; above the
threshold it sinks through R6 and turns Q3 (MMBT3906) on, which shorts Q2's gate to its source.

- Threshold without hysteresis: 2.495 x (1 + 53.6 / 10) = 15.87 V.
- R5 1 M from Q2's gate to REF adds hysteresis. Q2 on: the gate sits about 10 V below 12V_RP,
  rising trip **15.7 V**. Q2 off: the gate is pulled to 12V_RP by Q3, falling release **15.2 V**.
  (Equation: (Vin - 2.495) / R3 + (Vg - 2.495) / R5 = 2.495 / R4.)
- Margin to the ratings: TPS563208 VIN 17 V recommended, 19 V absolute (TPS56320x 5.1, 5.3);
  WRA1215S 18 V (datasheet input range). With 1 % resistors and the 0.5 % TL431 the rising trip
  stays within about 15.5 to 15.9 V.
- Q3 base current at the trip: (15.7 - 0.7 - 2) / 10 k = 1.3 mA, enough for the TL431 (1 mA
  minimum for regulation; here it only needs to saturate Q3). R6 is 0805: at a 30 V input it
  dissipates 27^2 / 10 k = 73 mW. R7 10 k holds Q3 off against the TL431's off-state current.
- Survives sustained inputs up to the FETs' 30 V (MMBT3906 40 V, TL431 36 V): a 19 V or 24 V
  adapter switches the device off. Above about 30 V nothing protects it.
- **C1 100 nF, Q2 gate to source:** soft start. With R2 the gate ramps with a 10 ms time constant,
  so the inrush into C2 (1000 uF plus 120 uF more on 12V_P) is about 12 V x 1.12 mF / 5 ms = 2.7 A
  instead of what the adapter and cable allow [Verify by simulation].
- The whole cut-off is to be simulated before layout (arch 9): trip, release, the linear region
  near the threshold, the zener's low-current knee (BZT52C10 is specified at 5 mA, here it runs at
  tens of uA, so |Vgs| may be 8 to 10 V, which moves the rising trip by about 0.1 V)
  [Verify by simulation].

**D3 SMBJ15A** (unidirectional, 15 V stand-off, 16.7 V minimum breakdown) after the switches, as
arch 9. Between 15.0 and 15.7 V (below the cut-off) it only leaks. Its clamp (about 24 V at full
pulse current) is above the buck's 19 V absolute maximum: it is for short transients on the cable,
which the 1000 uF also absorbs; sustained over-voltage is the cut-off's job [Verify].

**C2 1000 uF 25 V** electrolytic (10 x 10.5 SMD, Fit hand): arch 8's hold-up, 1000 uF x 1.5 V /
0.69 A = 2.2 ms from 10.5 V down to the module's 9 V minimum.

## 3. Isolated analogue supply (sheet `iso_supply`)

**PS1 WRA1215S-3WR2** (`rev_a:WRA1215S-3WR2`): **PIN NUMBERS UNVERIFIED** (the datasheet gives
them only in a drawing), stated on the sheet. Ctrl left open: "normal output when the Ctrl end is
suspended" (datasheet, Design reference 3). Input GND and output 0V both go to GND: the module is
used to make split rails, not for isolation.

Recommended circuit (Fig. 2, the 5 V and 12 V input column, read from the text extraction):

| Part | Fig. 2 | Here |
|---|---|---|
| C1, before Lin | 100 uF / 25 V | C30 100 uF 25 V electrolytic |
| Lin | 4.7 to 12 uH | L3 4.7 uH (SRN4018-4R7M) |
| C2, at the module input | 47 uF / 25 V | C31 47 uF 25 V electrolytic |
| C3, at each output | 10 to 22 uF / 50 V | C32, C34 10 uF 50 V X7R 1210 |
| Lout | 2.2 to 10 uH | L4, L5 10 uH (SRN4018-100M) |
| C4 / C5, after Lout | 100 uF / 50 V typical | C33, C35 100 uF 50 V electrolytic |
| Cd | 47 nF / 100 V | **omitted**: its position cannot be read from the text extraction [Verify Fig. 2] |

- **Lout 10 uH, not the architecture's 22 uH** (arch 5.3): 22 uH is outside the module's
  recommended 2.2 to 10 uH. With 100 uF the corner is 1 / (2 pi sqrt(10 uH x 100 uF)) = 5.0 kHz:
  at 250 kHz (5.3: PFM, full load) (250 / 5.0)^2 = 2470, -68 dB, so 100 to 150 mV pp of ripple
  (datasheet ripple and noise) is 40 to 60 uV at the LDO input, which then rejects at least 52 dB
  more. At 30 kHz (if PFM falls that far at idle) -31 dB [Verify on the bench, arch 5.3].
- **Capacitive load:** per output on D, 10 + 100 = 110 uF. The stricter limit is YLPTEC's 220 uF
  (arch 5.3; Mornsun allows 680 uF). Board A's LDO input capacitors on +15V_RAW and -15V_RAW must
  stay under 110 uF per rail [Verify with board A].
- **Hold-up (arch 8) recomputed:** arch 8 assumed 220 uF per rail. With 110 uF on D alone:
  110 uF x 2.4 V / 90.5 mA = 2.9 ms (worst load), 5.4 ms (typical 48.7 mA); with 12V_P's 2.2 ms
  the total is 5.1 ms worst against the relay's 3 ms release: margin about 2 ms, down from 5 ms.
  Board A's input capacitors add to it. Bring-up step 4 measures it [Verify].
- **Minimum load, R30 and R31, 3.0 k 1206, DNP.** The module needs 5 mA per output (selection
  guide: 100 / 5 mA). Board A's idle load is 29.2 mA typical per rail (arch 5.3: the op-amps'
  quiescent current), so in use they are not needed. They sit on the module side of JP3 and JP4,
  so they can be fitted if the module misbehaves unloaded during bring-up (15 V / 3.0 k = 5 mA,
  75 mW, 1206 rated 0.25 W).
- **JP3, JP4 (open):** as the bucks. Test points on +15V_FILT and -15V_FILT.
- Inductors: SRN4018 (4 x 4 mm), about 0.1 A DC on L4/L5 and 0.3 A on L3 (3.4 W / 0.8 / 12 V
  worst, arch 5.3) [Verify ratings of the chosen part].

## 4. Supervisor (sheet `supervisor`)

**U4 TPS3808G01DBVR** (`Power_Supervisor:TPS3808DBV`; SBVS050N). SENSE threshold 0.405 V
(adjustable version, 6.5), open-drain RESET, VDD 1.7 to 6.5 V. VDD from NUCLEO_3V3 (the
architecture's 3V3_DIG no longer exists on D, board split 1); RAILS_OK pulled up by R43 10 k to
NUCLEO_3V3 on D only, to PD4 (CN11-39, EXTI4) and J_LINK pin 22.

- **Divider:** R40 255 k (12V_P) / R41 10 k, 40.5 uA at the threshold (SENSE input current
  25 nA, negligible).
- **Hysteresis:** the built-in one is about 1 % (6.5), about 0.1 V at 12V_P. R42 2.8 M from
  RAILS_OK to SENSE adds positive feedback: RAILS_OK high (3.3 V) adds 1.03 uA into SENSE, so
  12V_P must fall further. **Falling: 10.47 V.** RAILS_OK low: R42 draws 0.15 uA, and the
  built-in hysteresis adds 1 %: **rising about 10.9 V.**
  Falling: 0.405 + (40.5 - 1.03) uA x 255 k = 10.47 V. Rising: 0.409 + (40.9 + 0.15) uA x 255 k
  = 10.88 V.
- Tolerance: VIT +/-1.25 % (G01, 6.5) and 1 % resistors, about +/-2.3 %: rising up to about
  11.15 V. A 12 V adapter at -5 % (11.4 V) minus the PTC and two FETs (about 0.15 V at 0.69 A)
  gives 11.25 V: it still releases RAILS_OK, with 0.1 V to spare [Verify the adapter; see open
  decision 4].
- **Delay:** C42 33 nF on CT: tD = 33 / 175 + 0.5 ms = **189 ms** (Equation 1). X7R, low leakage
  as 7.3.2 asks.
- C41 1 nF on SENSE (7.3.1: 1 to 10 nF); with 9.6 k source impedance, 10 us.
- MR left open (internal 90 k pull-up, 7.3.3).
- The output's load on board A changes the high level and so the hysteresis slightly; if A puts a
  pull-down on RAILS_OK it should be 100 k or more [Verify with board A].

## 8. Panel interface (sheet `panel`)

J6 `Conn_02x20_Odd_Even`, Value "J_PANEL", `IDC-Header_2x20_P2.54mm_Vertical` (shrouded box
header), pins from `links.pins("J_PANEL")`; pin 39 no-connect.

**POT_REF buffer** (board split 2.3).
- U8 TLV9001IDCKR (SC70-5; SBOS833R; the KiCad symbol's 1 IN+, 2 V-, 3 IN-, 4 OUT, 5 V+ is TI's
  DCK pin-out [Verify against Table 6-1, garbled in the extraction]).
- **Supplied from 5V_DIG, not NUCLEO_3V3 (deviation).** On the Nucleo VREF+ is tied to VDDA and VDD
  through R40 (UM2408 Table 16, "VDDA and VREF+ on STM32H7 are connected to VDD"), so VREFP
  equals NUCLEO_3V3. A follower supplied from 3.3 V cannot output 3.3 V into 2.3 mA: the
  TLV9001's output swings to within 35 mV of the rail into 2 k (7.10), plus the isolation
  resistor's drop. From 5 V it has 1.6 V of headroom. It cannot drive POT_REF above VREFP, since
  its input is VREFP (at most VDD), and at power-up 5V_DIG comes before NUCLEO_3V3 with VREFP at
  0 V. 5.0 V is inside its 1.8 to 5.5 V range.
- **Dual feedback for a capacitive load with no DC error:** R80 47 ohm isolation, C82 1 uF X7R
  on POT_REF to POT_GND, R81 10 k from POT_REF to IN- (DC feedback, so R80's 2.3 mA x 47 =
  0.11 V drop is corrected), C81 330 nF from OUT to IN- (high-frequency feedback around the op-amp
  alone). The load is capacitive whatever is fitted: each pot's 10 nF wiper capacitor on the
  panel (11.2) sits directly on POT_REF when that pot is at its top, up to 7 x 10 nF, so C82 is
  made to dominate. Rule used: R81 at least 100 x R80 (4.7 k), and C81 at least
  2 (Ro + R80) C_L / R81 = 2 x (1.2 k + 47) x 1.07 uF / 10 k = 0.27 uF, taking the open-loop
  output impedance Ro as 1.2 k (Figure 7-23 is a plot) [Verify Ro, and the margin by simulation].
  The DC error is the offset, 1.6 mV maximum (7.10), 2 LSB at 12 bits.
- C80 100 nF on U8's supply.
- **Bypass R83 0 ohm VREFP to POT_REF, DNP** (board split 2.3). **Fit R83 only with R80 removed**:
  otherwise U8, its feedback node shorted to its input, can drive VREFP through 47 ohm (said on
  the sheet).

**Pot wipers** (arch 11.2): R101 to R107 1 k in series, C101 to C107 100 nF to POT_GND at the MCU
end. Corner 1.6 kHz; MCU-side nets POT1_ADC to POT7_ADC.

**Buttons and encoders** (arch 11.3): on the ribbon side R111 to R122 10 k pull-ups to NUCLEO_3V3;
R131 to R142 1 k in series; at the pin C111 to C122 to GND. Buttons and both encoder push
switches 10 nF (press 1 k x 10 nF = 10 us, release 11 k x 10 nF = 110 us), encoder A and B 1 nF
(1 us and 11 us, for the timers' encoder inputs). MCU-side nets BTN1_MCU, ENC1_A_MCU, ENC1_SW_MCU
and so on.

**LEDs:** R151 to R157 150 ohm between the MCU pin (LED1_MCU to LED7_MCU) and the ribbon, the
series resistor at the MCU end that arch 9 asks for on every ribbon line. A ribbon line shorted to
PANEL_3V3 then sinks at most 3.3 V / 150 = 22 mA into the pin. **The panel's own LED resistor
must allow for it:** for 2.5 mA at a 2.0 V LED, (3.3 - 2.0) / 2.5 mA = 520 ohm in total, so 360 ohm
(E24) on the panel boards [coordinate with PL and PR; see open decision 3].

**I2C4** (arch 11.5): R162, R163 4.7 k pull-ups to NUCLEO_3V3 at the MCU end, and R160, R161
100 ohm in series towards the ribbon (arch 9; adds 0.07 V to the low level at 0.7 mA). Not
in the task list; added for consistency with arch 9.

**PANEL_3V3:** FB80 BLM18PG221SN1 (220 ohm at 100 MHz, about 1.4 A, about 0.05 ohm DC [Verify
Murata datasheet]) from NUCLEO_3V3, then C83 10 uF and C84 100 nF. Why: NUCLEO_3V3 is also VDDA
and VREF+ (above), so whatever the panel injects into it (LED PWM current edges, the OLED's charge
pump, pickup on 150 mm of ribbon) lands on the ADC's reference. The bead keeps the
high-frequency part on the panel side, and C83 supplies the PWM edges locally. Drop at 46 mA
(arch 5.4): about 2 mV. The bead's low-frequency inductance with 10 uF may peak somewhere in the
tens of kHz; its resistance damps it [Verify by measurement].

## Deviations from the architecture and the task, and why

1. **U8 (POT_REF buffer) supplied from 5V_DIG, not NUCLEO_3V3**: VREF+ = VDD on the Nucleo, so a
   3.3 V follower cannot reach it (section 8).
2. **Module output inductors 10 uH, not 22 uH** (arch 5.3): the module's recommended range is
   2.2 to 10 uH (Fig. 2). Still -68 dB at 250 kHz. Hold-up margin recomputed (section 3).
3. **LED series resistor on D** (150 ohm): arch 11.4 puts the whole LED resistor on the panel,
   arch 9 asks for a series resistor at the MCU end on every line; the task asked for it. The
   panel's resistor drops to about 360 ohm.
4. **I2C4 series resistors** (100 ohm), from arch 9; not in the task list.
5. **Supervisor supplied from NUCLEO_3V3** (arch 5.1 said 3V3_DIG, which is gone from D).
   Hysteresis added with R42 from the output: TPS3808 has only about 1 % of its own.
6. **BOOT0 header to VDD (CN11-5)**, not 3V3: the manual's own jumper position (section 5).
7. **Regulator links are open solder jumpers**: the board is inert until they are bridged
   (arch 14 allows either a 0 ohm link or a solder jumper).
8. **Bucks' EN from a divider** (100 k / 47 k): not in the architecture; keeps transients on
   EN under its rating.
9. **Cd (47 nF / 100 V) of the module's Fig. 2 omitted**: its position is not readable from the
   text extraction.
10. **No PWR_FLAG on GND**: PS1's 0V pin is a power output on GND, and a PWR_FLAG on the same net
    is an ERC error (power output to power output). GND is therefore "driven" by PS1 in ERC's
    eyes; it really arrives through J1. Removing PS1 later would need the flag back.
11. **Optical receiver supply through a ferrite bead**, transmitter direct (task: "local
    decoupling"); both DNP.

## Open owner decisions

1. **Rail links:** open solder jumpers (as drawn: nothing powers up until bridged by hand, which
   matches bring-up step 1), or 0 ohm 0805 links fitted by JLC (works out of the box, and step 1
   then means desoldering five links).
2. **PTC part** (above), and whether the 12 V adapter's tolerance is known: the supervisor's
   rising threshold (about 10.9 V, up to 11.15 V) needs at least about 11.3 V at J1.
3. **LED resistor split:** 150 ohm on D and about 360 ohm on the panel boards (PL, PR); or 0 ohm
   on D and the full 520 ohm on the panel, giving up arch 9's protection on those lines.
4. **POT_REF buffer from 5V_DIG** (deviation 1), or keep the stage 2 wiring (fit R83, remove R80
   and U8).
5. **J1 connector:** JST XH 4-way as drawn, or a screw terminal if the owner prefers to wire the
   jack and switch without crimping.
6. Still open from stage 2: JP2 to 5-6 and male headers in CN11/CN12, USB option A first, the
   single chassis bond and insulating standoffs (arch 17, items 5, 6, 12).

## Changes after the schematic review (2026-11-19)

- **M1, inrush:** C1 moved from Q2's gate-source to gate-drain and raised to 470 nF, so Q2 ramps
  12V_P at about 190 V/s (12 V in about 60 ms, about 0.22 A into the 1.17 mF downstream) instead
  of switching on hard. Q2 still dissipates about 84 mJ per plug-in (1/2 C V^2, independent of
  the ramp rate), about 1.4 W for 60 ms, so it is now an FDS9435A in SO-8 instead of the SOT-23
  DMP3099L. Q1 is unchanged (it is fully on before the ramp starts). Simulate trip, release and
  the ramp before layout [Verify].
- **M3, adapter margin:** R40 255 k to 237 k: RAILS_OK falls at 9.76 V and rises at about
  10.04 V (review computation), leaving about 1.4 V to a 12 V adapter at -5 %. Hold-up from 9.76 V
  still exceeds the relays' 3 ms release (review: about 5.4 ms) [measure at bring-up step 4].
- **m4:** 100 k pull-downs R56 to R58 on the three inputs board A drives (MCLK_PC9, DOUT1,
  CODEC_OVF), so they are defined with J_LINK unplugged; a sheet note marks J7 as live once the
  rail jumpers are bridged.
- **m7:** R84, 1 M from VREFP to GND, defines U8's input without the Nucleo. The supervisor's
  dependence on the Nucleo's 3V3 stays; the bring-up order now fits the Nucleo before the
  RAILS_OK measurements (architecture 15).
- **m9:** MCLK_MCU reaches PC9 through R55 (0 ohm) with C53 (10 pF, not fitted), room for a

- **m10:** sheet note: J2 and J3 on the bottom side.
